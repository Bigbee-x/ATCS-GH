# Streetscape textures

These original 1024 × 1024 JPEG texture maps are bundled for offline rendering.
All three assets are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
by their creators through [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license).

| Asset | Creator | Maps included | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Asphalt 02 | Rob Tuytel | Diffuse, displacement, roughness | https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02 |
| Plastered Wall 05 | Charlotte Baglioni | Diffuse, displacement, roughness | https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_05 |
| Dry Ground Rocks | Rob Tuytel | Diffuse, displacement, roughness | https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_rocks |

The maps are unmodified. The Godot material applies paint tints, world-space
projection, height-based bump detail and weather effects at runtime. The
displacement maps affect shading only, preserving road and collision geometry.

`manifest.json` records each original download URL, byte count and provider MD5
checksum, verified when downloaded. Total source texture size: 4,690,629 bytes.
No API key or internet connection is needed to run the visualizer.
